# AI-Assisted Tag Suggestions

Status: needs-triage
Priority: low

## Problem Statement

AI-generated tag suggestions could assist tagging, but the product boundary is undefined for privacy, provider, cost, and human review.

## Desired Outcome

Define whether and how TAGGER may analyze a photo and present proposed tags for explicit human approval.

## Open Triage Decisions

- Whether the feature should exist and which provider or local model is acceptable.
- Photo and metadata disclosure, consent, retention, and privacy expectations.
- Cost controls, credentials/configuration, failure behavior, and offline behavior.
- Suggestion review, approval, rejection, and relationship to existing tag mutations.

## Out of Scope

Implementing a provider integration before these product decisions are made.
